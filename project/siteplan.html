Site Name

Discover Ghana Culture introduces visitors to Ghana's traditions, creative arts, and history. The name makes the site's subject clear and welcomes curious readers.

Possible domain: discoverghanaculture.org

Site Purpose

The site is an inviting, easy-to-explore guide to Ghanaian culture. It shares stories and visual highlights about festivals, traditional attire, music and dance, food, and historic landmarks, helping students and travelers find meaningful background in one place.

Intended Audience

Students, travelers, and anyone interested in learning about Ghana's cultural heritage.

Scenarios

  • What are the major traditional festivals and celebrations in Ghana?
  • Where can I experience authentic Ghanaian music and dance?
  • What do the colors and patterns in Kente cloth represent?
  • Which historic landmarks should I learn about before visiting Ghana?

Site Structure

The homepage introduces the culture guide and highlights its main topics. Topic links lead visitors to focused information about each part of Ghanaian culture.

Body text: Open Sans. Comfortable, clear reading for stories and descriptions.
